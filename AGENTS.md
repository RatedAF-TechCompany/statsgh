
- Market/indicator widgets must label fixed-estimate sources and stale prices via `src/lib/dataProvenance.ts`, never present them as live — keeps numbers honest.
- Public article source links come from the `get_article_source` RPC, not direct reads of the admin-only newsroom table — preserves RLS.
- Market prices are written only by `market-data-refresh` (pg_cron hourly at :05, plus a GitHub Actions backup every 6 hours) from real feeds (open.er-api, FRED, GSE kwayisi), stamped with source + observation time; never insert estimates — keeps numbers honest.
- `newsroom-scan` and `newsroom-scheduled` authorise callers only via `_shared/scheduler-auth.ts` (service role key, `CRON_SECRET` env, the database scheduler token in `private.scheduler_auth` sent by pg_cron through `private.scheduler_headers()`, or a signed-in admin/editor); everything else gets 401 — no tokens in source or migrations.
- `newsroom-scheduled` runs the scan in the background (202) so callers never time out.
- Home, section and article pages use ISR (`revalidate = 120`) with on-demand refresh via `/api/revalidate`, called by the `articles_site_revalidate` DB trigger; every other page declares its own `dynamic = "force-dynamic"` — keeps new stories visible within ~2 minutes without rendering everything per request.
- Sitemaps are a `/sitemap.xml` index over `/sitemaps/pages.xml` and `/sitemaps/articles-N.xml` (5,000 URLs each, fetched with `.range()`), sharing `src/lib/sitemap.ts`; all public URLs use the www origin — avoids the 1,000-row cap.
- `backfill-images` handles at most 5 articles per run, one at a time with per-article timeouts, skipping failures — avoids WORKER_RESOURCE_LIMIT.
- Official Bank of Ghana figures (interbank FX, T-bills, policy rate, interbank rate) are written only by `bog-rates-scrape` into the `bog_*` tables via pg_cron with `private.scheduler_headers()`; failed scrapes write nothing and pages show the last stored value with its own date, falling back to the labelled open.er-api market mid-rate for FX only — keeps numbers official and honest.
